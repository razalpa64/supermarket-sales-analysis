# Empirical Supermarket Business Insights & Strategic Analysis

**Dataset Coverage**: 89 Calendar Days (January 1, 2019 – March 30, 2019)  
**Total Records Analyzed**: 1,000 transactions  
**Total Turnover Analyzed**: $322,966.75  

---

## 1. Merchandise Performance
- **Highest-Grossing Department**: **Food and beverages** generated **$56,144.84**, contributing **17.38%** of overall supermarket turnover.
- **Lowest-Grossing Department**: **Health and beauty** generated **$49,193.74**.
- **Highest Average Basket Value**: Orders in **Home and lifestyle** averaged **$336.64** per checkout.

## 2. Store Geography & Footfall
- **Leading Branch**: **Branch C (Naypyitaw)** leads all locations with **$110,568.71** (34.24% share).
- **Branch Parity**: Performance is tightly balanced across all 3 branches, with a maximum spread of only **4.12%** between top and bottom stores.

## 3. Payment Channels
- **Most Popular Channel**: **Ewallet** accounted for **34.5%** of transactions (345 orders).
- **Highest Ticket Channel**: **Cash** exhibited the largest average transaction value at **$326.18**.

## 4. Temporal Dynamics & Shopping Rhythm
- **Peak Hour**: Peak traffic occurs at **19:00 - 20:00** (113 checkouts).
- **Strongest Day**: **Saturday** yielded peak cumulative sales of **$56,120.81**.
- **Weekend vs. Weekday**: Weekend transactions averaged **$338.65** vs. **$316.34** on weekdays (a **7.05%** difference).

## 5. Loyalty & Membership
- **Membership Share**: Enrolled loyalty members account for **50.85%** of revenue.
- **Basket Comparison**: Members spend **$327.79** per visit vs. **$318.12** for non-members.

---

## 6. Scientific & Causal Clarification (Mandatory Disclaimer)
> **Descriptive Association vs. Causal Attribution**:
> The metrics above describe empirical associations observed in historical transactions. 
> For instance, while transactions using Cash exhibit higher average tickets, this does **not** prove that offering this payment method *causes* shoppers to purchase more. High-value shoppers may simply prefer electronic payment mechanisms. Decision-makers should not initiate major capital investments based solely on descriptive correlations without controlled A/B experimentation.
